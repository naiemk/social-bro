# Agency Services Agreement

This Agency Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date] by and between:

- **Client:** [Trustless Commerce legal entity], with notice email at [Client Email] and address at [Client Address]
- **Agency / Provider:** [Provider legal entity], with notice email at [Provider Email] and address at [Provider Address]

Client and Provider may each be referred to as a "Party" and together as the "Parties."

## 1. Purpose

Provider will supply content operations, social media marketing, SEO/content production, and customer support workflow services for the Trustless Commerce product, website, and related channels using a human-reviewed AI-assisted workflow.

## 2. Services

Provider will perform the services described in the Statement of Work attached below, including:

- content planning,
- social draft generation,
- SEO article and landing-page drafting,
- short-form video concepting and rendering workflow management,
- first-line support operations,
- queue management and escalation support,
- reporting and optimization.

## 3. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of [Initial Term], unless earlier terminated under this Agreement. After the initial term, the Agreement renews on a [monthly] basis unless either Party gives [30] days' written notice of non-renewal.

## 4. Fees and Payment

Client shall pay Provider:

- a recurring fee of **[Monthly Retainer]** per month, or
- the project fee listed in the Statement of Work, if applicable.

Invoices are due within **[Net 7 / Net 15 / Net 30]** days of invoice date. Late amounts may accrue interest at the lesser of **1.5% per month** or the highest rate allowed by law.

Client is responsible for third-party tool costs unless otherwise stated, including but not limited to:

- AI video generation credits,
- hosting and VPS costs,
- model-provider usage fees,
- communication platform fees,
- analytics and ad spend.

## 5. Approval Model

The Parties agree that Provider may use AI-assisted systems to generate drafts, scripts, replies, SEO assets, and short-form video plans. Final publication authority remains with Client unless explicitly delegated in writing.

The default operating model is:

- low-risk comments and FAQ responses may be auto-approved under agreed thresholds,
- higher-risk content is queued for Client approval,
- legal, billing, refund, fraud, and sensitive support issues must be escalated.

Client acknowledges that delays in approval may delay publication or support resolution.

## 6. Client Responsibilities

Client will:

- provide accurate product, policy, and brand information,
- identify prohibited claims and compliance boundaries,
- provide credentials, access, and assets needed for operation,
- review queued items within the agreed turnaround window,
- remain responsible for final legal/compliance approval of claims.

## 7. Intellectual Property

Upon full payment of all amounts due, final deliverables specifically created for Client under this Agreement are assigned to Client, excluding:

- Provider pre-existing tools,
- templates,
- prompts,
- workflows,
- automation logic,
- internal methods,
- general know-how.

Provider retains ownership of its background technology and reusable systems, including the `social-bro` orchestration framework, prompts, queueing systems, and deployment patterns, unless separately assigned in writing.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each Party shall keep the other Party's non-public business, technical, and commercial information confidential and use it only to perform under this Agreement.

## 9. Compliance and Claims

Provider will not knowingly publish content that makes unauthorized legal, regulatory, financial, or performance claims. Client remains responsible for:

- legal review,
- compliance approval,
- public policy decisions,
- product accuracy of final claims.

Provider may pause or escalate any request that appears deceptive, non-compliant, or unsafe.

## 10. Warranties Disclaimer

Except as expressly stated, services are provided "as is." Provider does not guarantee:

- ranking position,
- audience growth,
- revenue outcomes,
- conversion outcomes,
- platform algorithm performance,
- uninterrupted third-party services.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent allowed by law, neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages. Provider's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client to Provider in the [3 / 6] months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 12. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement:

- for convenience upon [30] days' written notice, or
- immediately for material breach if the breach is not cured within [10] days after notice.

Upon termination:

- Client shall pay for services rendered through the termination date,
- Provider will deliver completed paid deliverables,
- access credentials should be rotated or revoked by Client.

## 13. Independent Contractor

Provider is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of Client.

## 14. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [Jurisdiction], excluding conflict-of-law principles.

## 15. Entire Agreement

This Agreement and the Statement of Work constitute the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter and supersede prior discussions on that subject.
