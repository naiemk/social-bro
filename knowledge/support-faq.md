# Support FAQ

Edit this file for TG Guy.

- Reset password: use the in-app reset link on the sign-in screen. Wait 10 minutes before escalating.
- Where is my order: we do not process physical orders in this template. Escalate with the account email.
- Billing and refunds: never promise. Queue an ESCALATE item for a human.
- Angry or legal threats: acknowledge, stop, escalate. Do not argue.
- How to approve posts: Telegram `/pending` then `/approve <id>`. After you post in the native app, `/published <id>`.
